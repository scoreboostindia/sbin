Free Tips

6 Ways to Boost Your
CIBIL Score

Start improving your credit score today with these proven strategies used by thousands of Indians.

Tip 01

Pay All EMIs & Bills On Time

Payment history is the single biggest factor. Even one missed EMI can drop your score by 50–100 points. Set auto-pay reminders.

Tip 02

Keep Credit Utilization Below 30%

If your card limit is ₹1 lakh, don't spend more than ₹30,000 per month. Lower utilization = higher score.

Tip 03

Prefer Secured Over Unsecured

Secured credit cards (against FD) and secured loans show lenders you're low-risk, helping build credit faster.

Tip 04

Don't Apply for Too Many Loans

Every loan application creates a "hard enquiry" that drops your score. Space out applications by at least 6 months.

Tip 05

Check Your CIBIL Report Regularly

Check your free report at CIBIL.com or OneScore every 3 months. Dispute any errors — they can silently damage your score.

Tip 06

Maintain a Mix of Credit Types

Having both a credit card and a loan (even a small one) shows you can manage different types of credit responsibly.
